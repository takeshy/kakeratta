ACCOUNT LINKING
Enable the skill and start account linking in the Alexa app.
Select “Review access / 審査用ログイン” on the Kakeratta login screen.
Sign in with the supplied credentials and approve linking.

LAUNCH AND HELP
Say “Alexa, open Bits Notes” for a short greeting.
Say “Help” for instructions about notes, replies and schedules.

READ AND RESPOND TO A REPLY
Say “Read my replies.” The latest reply is read aloud. An English sample reply is provided.
Say “Reply”, then “Thank you. I will try that” when prompted.
Alexa reads back the text. Say “Yes” to save it in the same conversation.

CREATE AND CORRECT A NOTE
Say “Take a note”, then “I want to go for a walk tomorrow” when prompted.
Alexa reads back the text without saving it yet.
Say “No”, then “I want to go for a walk on Saturday” when prompted.
After the new read-back, say “Yes”. Only the confirmed text is saved.
You can also say “Let me try again” to replace the draft.

CANCEL BEFORE POSTING
Say “Take a note”, then “This is a cancellation test” when prompted.
At the confirmation question, say “Cancel”. The session ends without saving the draft.

CHECK SCHEDULES
Reopen the skill and say “Read my schedule”. Upcoming plans saved in Kakeratta are read aloud.
An English example plan is provided. If its date has passed, the skill may report no upcoming plans.
The skill does not use the Alexa Reminders API, fetch external calendars, or automatically notify you through Alexa at the scheduled time.

EXIT AND VERIFY SAVED CONTENT
Say “Stop” to end the session.
Open https://kakeratta.net/?login=1&review=1&lang=en and sign in with the same credentials to inspect saved notes, replies and cancellation results.

Replies to new notes are asynchronous and may take time to arrive.
Within the same voice session, “Read my replies” refers to the conversation you last posted or replied to.
If a session ends between tests, reopen the skill before continuing.
